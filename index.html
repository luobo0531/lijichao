李吉超 研究员

Dr. Li Jichao

清华大学博士 | 博导 | 中国地震局工程力学研究所研究员

国家级奖项获得者 | 防灾减灾领域专家

Ph.D. from Tsinghua University | Supervisor | Researcher at Institute of Engineering Mechanics, China Earthquake Administration

National Award Winner | Expert in Disaster Prevention and Mitigation

李吉超研究员深耕于防灾减灾、土木工程及地震工程领域，拥有丰富的学术研究经验和工程实践能力。作为清华大学博士、博士生导师，他致力于推动结构工程领域的技术创新与发展，在先进混合试验技术、功能可恢复韧性结构、抗震韧性评估等方面取得了显著成果。

其研究团队开发了多项具有自主知识产权的科研软件工具，涵盖动力实验数据处理、人工波反应谱分析、供水管网/变电站地震分析等多个领域，为我国的防震减灾事业做出了重要贡献。

Dr. Li Jichao has dedicated his career to disaster prevention and mitigation, civil engineering, and earthquake engineering. With a Ph.D. from Tsinghua University and extensive experience as a supervisor, he is committed to advancing technological innovation in structural engineering, particularly in areas such as advanced hybrid testing techniques, functional recoverable resilient structures, and seismic resilience assessment.

His research team has developed numerous proprietary scientific software tools covering dynamic experimental data processing, artificial wave response spectrum analysis, and seismic analysis of water supply networks and substations, making significant contributions to China's disaster prevention and mitigation efforts.
