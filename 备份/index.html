李吉超 副研究员

Dr. Li Jichao

工学博士 | 硕士生导师 | 中国地震局工程力学研究所副研究员

1990年2月出生 | 云南昆明人

Ph.D. | Master's Supervisor | Associate Researcher at Institute of Engineering Mechanics, China Earthquake Administration

Born in February 1990 | Kunming, Yunnan

李吉超，工学博士，副研究员，硕士生导师。主要从事工程系统抗震韧性分析方法与提升技术、智能振动控制理论与装置、重大工程地震灾害风险监测与评估研究。

主持、参与国家自然科学基金面上项目1项、青年科学基金1项和黑龙江省自然科学基金联合引导项目2项等10项科研项目，发表论文40余篇，其中SCI/EI检索30篇，获得国家发明专利6项，美国发明专利1项，实用新型7项，软件著作权14项，参与编写规范6项，担任国家自然科学基金项目通讯评审专家，担任《Reliability Engineering & System Safety》、《Engineering Structures》、《Earthquake Engineering and Engineering Vibration》、《建筑结构学报》《工程力学》、《地震工程与工程振动》等期刊审稿人。

Dr. Li Jichao is an associate researcher and master's supervisor. He mainly engages in research on seismic resilience analysis methods and improvement technologies for engineering systems, intelligent vibration control theory and devices, and risk monitoring and assessment of earthquake disasters for major projects.

He has presided over and participated in 10 scientific research projects, including 1 general project of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, 1 youth science fund project, and 2 joint guidance projects of the Heilongjiang Provincial Natural Science Foundation. He has published more than 40 papers, including 30 indexed by SCI/EI, obtained 6 national invention patents, 1 US invention patent, 7 utility model patents, 14 software copyrights, and participated in compiling 6 specifications. He serves as a communication review expert for National Natural Science Foundation projects and a reviewer for journals such as 《Reliability Engineering & System Safety》, 《Engineering Structures》, 《Earthquake Engineering and Engineering Vibration》, 《Journal of Building Structures》, 《Engineering Mechanics》, and 《Earthquake Engineering and Engineering Vibration》.
